# Pt/Pturb

This is Basic Block Catalog type `407` (`Pt/Pturb`). It represents a mechanical or drive-train relation used by rotating-machine models.

<!-- veragrid-block-introduction:start -->
**Pt/Pturb** belongs to an electromechanical machine or prime-mover model. It links electrical torque and flux with rotor speed, angle, mechanical power, or actuator dynamics, making it central to frequency, voltage, and rotor-angle stability studies.

## Typical use

- Use it when electrical transients must interact with rotating mass or machine controls.
- Initialize torque, power, flux, and speed consistently with the solved power flow.
<!-- veragrid-block-introduction:end -->

## Behaviour

- Library location: `Native / Mechanical / Drive Train`.
- Inputs: 3.
- Outputs: 1.
- Declared states: 0.
- Configurable parameters: 1.
- The imported definition is fully supported by the Dynamic Editor catalogue.

## Characteristic equations

$$
0 = (pt - (proc_{select\_1} \cdot \frac{pturb \cdot PN}{sgnn \cdot cosn} + (1 - proc_{select\_1}) \cdot pturb))
$$

$$
pturb(t_0) = (proc_{selfix\_0} \cdot \frac{pt \cdot sgnn \cdot cosn}{PN} + (1 - proc_{selfix\_0}) \cdot pt)
$$
