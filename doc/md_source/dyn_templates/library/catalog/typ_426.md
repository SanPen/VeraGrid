# Shaft i-J

This is Basic Block Catalog type `426` (`Shaft i-J`). It represents a mechanical or drive-train relation used by rotating-machine models.

<!-- veragrid-block-introduction:start -->
**Shaft i-J** belongs to an electromechanical machine or prime-mover model. It links electrical torque and flux with rotor speed, angle, mechanical power, or actuator dynamics, making it central to frequency, voltage, and rotor-angle stability studies.

## Typical use

- Use it when electrical transients must interact with rotating mass or machine controls.
- Initialize torque, power, flux, and speed consistently with the solved power flow.
<!-- veragrid-block-introduction:end -->

## Behaviour

- Library location: `Native / Mechanical / Drive Train`.
- Inputs: 1.
- Outputs: 1.
- Declared states: 0.
- Configurable parameters: 5.
- The imported definition is fully supported by the Dynamic Editor catalogue.

## Characteristic equations

$$
xdtheta_{ji}(t_0) = (\frac{1}{K_{ji}} \cdot -D_{jj} \cdot xomega_{j})
$$

$$
xomega_{j}(t_0) = omega_{i}
$$
